1. Scope and Operator

This Privacy Policy explains how Khristian Boone, also known as sharpsplinter [351311] ("Publisher," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with Torn Company Assistant ("TCA" or the "App"). It applies to the Windows desktop App and support communications with Publisher.

The App is designed as a local desktop application. As of the effective date, it does not use a Publisher-operated backend, advertising network, remote telemetry service, or licensing server. Information is nevertheless processed locally and transmitted to the third-party services you connect.

2. Information the App Processes

2.1 Credentials and configuration

The App processes Torn limited-access API keys, optional Torn public API keys, optional Tornstats API keys, Google OAuth credentials and refresh tokens, the path to your Google OAuth desktop-client configuration, Google Sheet identifiers and display names, company names, position settings, employee position locks, visible-column preferences, and the last selected company.

2.2 Torn company and employee information

Depending on the features you run and the access authorized by the API key, the App processes company profile and financial information, inventory and sales information, employee names and Torn IDs, positions, wages, days in company, last-action timestamps, work stats, effectiveness components, and company-type information. It may derive turnover events, wage-efficiency indicators, health scores, stockout estimates, position projections, and proposed assignments.

2.3 Google user data

The App requests only the Google OAuth drive.file scope. This permits access only to Google Sheets the App creates or that you explicitly select through Google Picker. The App does not request the broad Google Sheets spreadsheet scope or access to unrelated Drive files. It reads and writes only the company-data tabs in the selected or App-created Sheet.

The App stores the resulting OAuth credential locally so it can refresh authorization and perform user-requested and scheduled operations. It does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, sale, generalized artificial-intelligence model training, or unrelated purposes.

2.4 Local diagnostic information

The App writes a local efficiency-verification log in its installation directory. This log may contain employee names, Torn IDs, company-type identifiers and names, matching methods, position names, and projected position values. The log is not automatically transmitted to Publisher.

2.5 Support communications

If you contact Publisher, Publisher may receive the contact information, message content, attachments, and diagnostic details you choose to provide. Do not send API keys, OAuth tokens, or unnecessary employee information in support requests.

3. How Information Is Used

The App uses information to authenticate requests you direct; retrieve authorized Torn and Tornstats data; create, read, update, and organize Google Sheets; calculate and display company metrics; preserve your local settings; run scheduled tasks; diagnose projection matching; prevent configuration errors; and provide requested support.

Publisher does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use Google Workspace data for advertising. The App contains no third-party advertising SDK.

4. Google API Services User Data

TCA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing Google Sheets and Drive-file functionality described in this Policy.

The App does not transfer Google user data to Publisher-controlled servers. Transfers occur to Google as necessary to authenticate and perform the Sheet or Drive-file action you request. Google may process information under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

5. How Information Is Shared

Information is disclosed to Torn when the App makes Torn API requests, to Tornstats when the App requests position-efficiency projections, and to Google when the App authenticates or reads and writes Google Sheets and related Drive files. Each provider independently processes information under its own terms.

Data written to a Google Sheet is accessible to the Google account owner and anyone with whom that owner shares the Sheet. You control those sharing permissions. Publisher may disclose support information if required by law, to protect rights or security, or in connection with a business transfer, subject to applicable legal requirements.

6. Local Storage and Security

The App stores Google OAuth credentials, Torn and Tornstats API keys, and company configuration in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Knotty Oil Tracker\secrets.dpapi. That file is encrypted using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI) and is intended to be usable only by the same Windows account on the same machine.

DPAPI encryption reduces exposure but cannot guarantee security. Anyone who can operate your unlocked Windows account may be able to use the App's credentials. You are responsible for Windows account security, backups, Google Sheet permissions, and credential revocation. Legacy plaintext files may remain until removed, and file deletion is not guaranteed secure erasure.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local credentials and settings remain until you remove or replace them, delete the DPAPI secrets file, or remove the relevant Windows profile. Local diagnostic logs remain until you delete them. Google Sheets and their history remain under your Google account's retention and sharing controls until you edit or delete them. Torn and Tornstats retain information according to their own policies.

To remove App-controlled local data, disconnect or revoke Google access, revoke or rotate Torn and Tornstats keys, close the App, delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Knotty Oil Tracker\secrets.dpapi, and delete the App's logs directory. To remove cloud data, delete or edit the relevant Google Sheets and empty trash where appropriate. Uninstalling the executable alone may not remove these files or cloud records.

You can revoke the App's Google authorization through your Google Account's third-party access settings. Revocation prevents future access but does not automatically delete Sheets or local logs already created.
